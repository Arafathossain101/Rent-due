Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 6th September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Rent Record mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Rent Record is designed as an offline application. All data you enter into the app is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your data on external servers. The data stored on your device includes:

  • Property details (house names, floor counts, room numbers).
  • Financial records (rent, gas, electricity, custom bills, and payment histories).
  • Renter information (names and identification documents).

3. Device Permissions

To function correctly, Rent Record requires the following device permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Required to capture and attach images of properties and renter identification documents.
  • File Storage: Required to upload PDF identification documents and permanently save captured images to the app's internal storage directory.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because all data remains on your local device, we do not share, sell, or disclose your personal or financial data to any third parties.

5. Data Security & Loss

Your data is secured by your device's native operating system protections. Because data is stored locally, uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data will permanently delete your records. We recommend keeping your device secured with a passcode or biometric lock.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
